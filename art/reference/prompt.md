# Reference provenance

`factions-and-battlefield.png` is the generated visual reference supplied during this project. This record summarizes the visible image and intended direction; it does not reproduce the exact generation prompt, which was not available to this planning agent.

The sheet shows a painterly fantasy RTS on parchment, with a fixed isometric battlefield below two faction rosters. The orcs have olive skin, bulky silhouettes, rough timber, dark iron, red roofs and orange lamps. Their four units are a tool-carrying worker, axe-and-shield infantry, crossbow skirmisher and drum berserker. Their buildings are a great hall, lumber depot with a crane, fortified barracks and watchtower.

The fairies have thin silhouettes, prominent veined wings, teal leaves, pale petals, amber lamps and violet magic. Their units are a gardener, spear guardian, moth archer and illusion-weaver. Their buildings are an ancient inhabited tree, floral nectar grove, blossom pavilion and thorn spire holding violet crystals. The game's Moonwell corresponds to the floral nectar grove; its name does not imply a stone-well model.

The battlefield has olive grass, worn dirt paths, evergreens and broadleaf trees, pale rocks with warm ore veins, scattered flowers, and a circular ruined stone clearing. The reference establishes material, silhouette and lighting direction. Game assets must be generated in Blender after the complete placeholder gameplay loop is proven. The reference itself is not a finished sprite atlas.

See `docs/ART_PIPELINE.md` for game ID mapping, camera calibration, export dimensions, animation inventory and reproducibility requirements.
