# Economy and settlements evidence

This checkout implements features 11–20 through paid commands, physical actors and finite stock. `src/core/economy-types.ts` defines the saved state and commands. `src/core/economy.ts` handles forestry, economic structures, caravan recruitment and local specialization. `src/core/economy-cargo.ts` handles cargo, markets, raids, salvage and neutral delivery contracts. `EconomyTools` sends commands through the same recorded simulation entry point used by the battlefield.

Grove planting costs 8 wood. A worker must reach the tile and plant for four seconds; the sapling then grows for sixty seconds and provides 100 wood. Fire destroys immature groves. A deep mine costs 180 wood, 200 ore and 20 crystal and extends one depleted ore site by half its original deposit, up to 800 ore. Its site cannot be upgraded twice. An extractor costs 100 wood, 90 ore and 15 crystal. Nearby crystal workers harvest at 1.15 times normal speed, or 1.9 times when overcharged. Each twelve-second incident interval has a seeded 50 percent chance of 75 extractor damage. Ordinary worker repairs pay wood and restore its health.

A caravan costs 90 wood and 35 ore, recruits in eighteen seconds and carries 90 resources. It takes cargo from owned storage when it reaches the source and transfers it at an owned or visible allied destination. Trade income arrives with the cargo and consumes finite public market crystal. A destroyed caravan drops its remaining stock. Neutral market transactions transfer finite commodity and currency stock atomically; demand raises quotes, recovery reduces demand, and an integrated price curve prevents splitting purchases to bypass the quote.

Raids transfer up to 24 resources into a military unit's cargo after a three-second channel. Workers collect up to 18 resources from finite salvage after a two-second channel. Both must reach owned storage before resources enter that stockpile. Killing a carrier drops its remaining cargo. Paid buildings and siege engines leave a bounded fraction of their paid materials; free starting actors, illusions and summons do not create new salvage. Local warehouse stocks hold up to 900 resources and require an ordered caravan delivery before they enter central spendable resources. Expansion headquarters choose one paid specialization. The nearest completed headquarters defines the local region: mining speeds ore gathering, military speeds recruitment, and research speeds research. Other bases keep their original rates.

Each neutral village has a finite reward pool. Its contracts request a resource, amount, destination and deadline. Completed deliveries consume the offered cargo and pay the promised reward once. Expired and incomplete contracts do not pay a full reward. Accepting a contract or trading at a market requires an owned real unit nearby. Enemy warehouse stock, cargo, route endpoints, contract assignments and ledger values stay private.
