## Current construction and support rules

The [Necropolis definition](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/core/faction-systems-content.ts:9) calls its effect consecration of nearby territory. Its radius is 6; cost is 120 wood, 50 ore and 25 crystal; health is 550; build time is 28 seconds. The implementation converts the local area into supporting ground through a building aura. It does not change terrain tile kinds for a Necropolis.

The [construction reason](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/core/faction-systems.ts:59) requires the correct faction, an owned worker on the selected level, finite in-bounds visible coordinates and sufficient resources. The [command implementation](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/core/faction-systems.ts:81) uses the placement hook, pays once, creates the foundation, records its cost and assigns build orders. The [placement hook](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/core/simulation.ts:85) calls [canPlace](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/core/simulation.ts:184), which checks age, definition, level, footprint visibility, buildable terrain and physical conflicts. None checks world-site ownership, captured region ownership or a prior capture action.

The [support rule](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/core/faction-systems.ts:116) requires a live non-illusion raised unit and a live completed canonical Necropolis allied with it, on the same level, at distance at most 6. Each step adds `dt` to the troop's absolute expiry and heals by `dt*2` capped at maximum health. It reads no captured-site ownership. Consequently the aura supports a legal source on captured or uncaptured ground alike. This is a statement about the implementation, not a new demand that captured ground must be the only legal location.

Relic capture is a separate [neutral-world operation](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/core/neutral-world.ts:196). After 10 seconds uncontested within 2.4 tiles it assigns `site.owner=site.capturing`; its consequence is the [eight-tile, 12% damage bonus](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/core/neutral-world.ts:240). It does not create or activate a Necropolis support zone.

The [Necropolis panel](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/ui/FactionTools.ts:56) instructs selected workers to build at a destination and describes healing and lifetime support. Its [command draft](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/ui/FactionTools.ts:68) supplies worker IDs, kind and point, with no captured-site selection. The [faction overlay](/home/morgana/Projects/orcs-vs-Fairies/src/game/FactionOverlay.ts:10) draws the selected owned building's six-tile radius. It does not show a world-site ownership relation.

## Scoped recommendation

Root can cite the passed local sustain/healing tests, real-summon support on both levels, paid worker construction and the independent capture capability now. I recommend retaining ID28 `in-progress` until there is executed evidence connecting a capture action to Necropolis support on the captured ground, if root keeps the captured-territory clause literal. One isolated capture/build/summon-support check would address that gap; this recommendation does not require rerunning or completing the whole native faction recipe.

If root considers a freely placed consecration aura on already-secured ground sufficient and accepts the composition of separately passed operations, root can make that scope decision explicitly. The evidence should still say that the joint capture-to-support encounter was unrun and that Necropolis behavior does not depend on site ownership. Source-loss expiry, recapture deactivation, changing terrain tile kinds and captured-only placement should not be introduced as new original acceptance requirements.
