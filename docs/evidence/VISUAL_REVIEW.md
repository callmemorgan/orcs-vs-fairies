# Final world artwork review

The generated reference preceded the placeholder gameplay gate and Blender production. The retained reference is `art/reference/factions-and-battlefield.png`; production provenance and the complete render validation are in `ASSET_REPRODUCTION.md`. All 31 scoped assets are integrated, with eight directional animated unit sets, eight building sets and fifteen terrain/resource/decoration assets.

The normal orc match recorded in `ORCS_PACED_MATCH.md` used the regenerated production assets at 1920×1080 and gameplay zoom 1. Earlier full-art fairy play is recorded in `FAIRIES_FINAL_ART_MATCH.md`. These are actual matches, supplemented by inspection of `art/blender/raw/units/roster-contact.png` and `directions-contact.png` after regeneration.

Orcs have broad green bodies, dark metal armor, red roof/cap accents and heavy equipment. Fairies have slender bodies, translucent wings, leaf clothing and gold/purple accents. They remain distinct in mixed combat. Orc role cues include the Ironjaw's shield and horned helmet, the Boltspitter's crossbow and cap, and the Wardrum's drum. Fairy workers have short wings; Thornblades have taller wings and a spear, Mothbows have pale wings and bows, and Veilweavers have large purple wings and long skirts. These features are visible in the eight-direction sheet as well as the match views.

The sprites use consistent upper-left lighting, soft shading and irregular material color patches. The buildings share the unit factions' materials and colors. Grass, dirt paths, stone ruins, ore and trees form a coherent fixed-isometric battlefield. The map has repeated terrain patterns, but no scoped world asset remains a placeholder.

Normal play showed workers traveling between deposits and buildings, moving troops changing direction, weapon attacks, foundations becoming complete structures, and visible fairy illusion copies. Structural validation separately covers all 1,536 unit frames and 40 building frames; it does not replace these in-game observations. The minimap and explored/visible fog states updated as the army advanced. In the orc match, the central ruins became dim after the army moved beyond them, while enemy defenders appeared as the advancing troops gained vision.

There are limits to readability. The Wardrum's drum is hidden in its exact rear view. Fairy workers and Thornblades share their base body and teal palette, so wing size and equipment carry their role distinction. Tall trees and roofs can obscure nearby units; F2, control groups, ground-based drag selection and selection/health indicators help the player command them. These are recorded art/readability limits, not claims of perfect visibility from every overlapping arrangement.

This review covers the final artwork and observed presentation. Subsequent core movement and AI fixes still need gameplay verification; visual approval does not close those requirements.
