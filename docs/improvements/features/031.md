# 031: Orc matchup tuning

The requirement is to rebalance Orc matchups with measured evidence and qualified conclusions. Ironjaw health changes from 175 to 165; Boltspitter attack cooldown changes from 1.5 to 1.4 seconds. Costs, armor, Fury caps, and special units remain unchanged. Definitions feed recruitment, observed content and selection statistics in browser and terminal matches.

The smaller change trades melee durability for ranged cadence. A broader candidate (190 melee health, 16 melee damage and 1.4 ranged cooldown) increased Orc survivor health across every opponent in the isolated sample, so it was rejected.

## Measurements

The committed `scripts/combat/measure.ts` runs the current content, then restores the two pre-031 values and repeats the same workload. At this feature commit this compares the foundation combat with the two-number change. After later features, its baseline means current rules with those two values restored, not the entire historical foundation.

There are 90 fights per version: five opposing factions, both starting sides, three position offsets, and melee, ranged, and mixed melee/ranged armies. Armies spend up to the same 1200-point budget (wood + ore + twice crystal). Every row records actual resource costs and unspent budget is implicit in those costs; this weighting is a comparison method, not a claim of resource exchange rates. Fights use grass, real attacks and attack-move, 0.05-second ticks and a 120-second cap. Simultaneous army deaths are draws, with no tiebreak. Offsets are small translations of the same formation, not independent maps or player strategies.

| Opponent | Orc W/L/D before | Orc W/L/D after | Mean surviving Orc hull + shield before / after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fairies | 12/6/0 | 12/6/0 | 351.9 / 285.1 |
| Dwarves | 18/0/0 | 18/0/0 | 535.2 / 479.4 |
| Undead | 4/14/0 | 8/10/0 | 25.1 / 41.3 |
| Tideborn | 12/3/3 | 18/0/0 | 274.3 / 256.8 |
| Automata | 18/0/0 | 18/0/0 | 421.6 / 364.2 |

No isolated fight timed out. In particular, the six ranged-versus-Undead fights change from Orc losses to wins; melee-versus-Undead remains six losses. The Tideborn win increase is a balance concern despite lower average survivor health. The results support a bounded shift in Orc roles, not equal faction strength.

Twenty full AI matches also ran through the normal economy, recruitment, research and combat loop: before and after against five factions, both starting sides, seed 4127, small map, 0.05-second ticks, 25-minute cap. Both versions had nine Orc wins and one loss (Dwarves against Orcs on side 1); all completed through HQ destruction without draws or timeouts. The narrow tuning did not change winners in this sample. This is one deterministic seed and map size, with no human matches. Larger maps, more seeds, specialists, micro and human balance remain unproven.
