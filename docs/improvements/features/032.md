# 032: Faction-specific battlefield roles

The requirement is faction-distinct cavalry, pikes and siege mechanics or statistics. All eighteen expanded-role definitions now use permanent faction profiles with the existing costs, age requirements, art IDs and role counters. Orcs trade speed for hull and armor; Fairies trade durability for movement, reach and siege range; Dwarves have heavy armor; Undead are lighter and faster; Tideborn keep wet-ground mobility; Automata expanded roles gain rechargeable shields. Descriptions and the existing recruitment, selection and observed-content displays read these same definitions.

The profile type excludes identity, costs, age and unit-counter fields, preventing a profile from accidentally replacing them. Building damage multipliers remain profile-defined (Fairy siege 4.6×, Dwarf siege 4.2×, other siege 4×). Cavalry retains 1.7× damage against ranged troops and pikes retain 3× against cavalry.

## Measurements and limits

`scripts/combat/measureRoles.ts` compares eighteen fixed 30-second duels per version on grass: cavalry against its faction's ranged troop, pike against cavalry, and siege against melee. Both units' costs are unchanged between versions and recorded in every row. Opposing cavalry also uses the old profile in the baseline. Each side has one unit; opposing roles have different costs, so this measures specialization at the same before/after cost, not equal-budget faction strength. The fixed endpoint includes shield recharge after combat. No map, human-match or AI balance study is claimed.

Those are balance risks to evaluate across armies, terrain and player decisions; neither balanced factions nor improved human play is promised.
